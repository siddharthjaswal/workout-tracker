Week 1 back from the break

  • 2 sets per exercise instead of 3-4.
  • One dumbbell size down from the listed start (10 → 7.5, 7.5 → 5, 5 stays 5 with fewer reps).
  • Stop every set 3 reps shy of failure. Soreness on day 2-3 is the enemy this week, not the weight.
  • Week 2: full sets, listed weights.

This week

Sat and Sun are the anchors — together they hit every muscle. Tue and Fri add the second dose. Office days are walk days.

Progression rule

  • Every exercise lists a start weight and a rep range. 2×10 means 10 kg in each hand, 1×10 one dumbbell.
  • Work inside the range. When every set hits the top of the range with 1-2 reps still in the tank → next dumbbell, reps drop back to the bottom.
  • Ladder: 5 → 7.5 → 10. At 10 kg and the top of the range, follow the exercise’s Progress line (slower lowering, pauses, 1.5 reps, harder variation, extra set).
  • Form first. A rep that needs a swing, a shrug or an arch doesn’t count.
  • Presses and squats will cap out at 10 kg within ~6-8 weeks. A 12.5 kg pair is the single best upgrade when that happens.

Missed a day?

  • Missed Tue → do Upper B on Wed or Thu evening if you can. Otherwise it’s gone.
  • Missed Fri → gone. Sat/Sun stay Upper A / Lower A, always. Never shift the anchor.
  • Never stack two lifting sessions on one day.
  • Busy evening → do the first 4 exercises of Upper B / Lower B and stop. 30 minutes beats zero.
  • The cycle resets every Saturday no matter what happened.

Fat loss, honestly

  • Love handles, belly and chest fat come off through a calorie deficit + daily steps, not through exercises aimed at them. No weighted side bends — they thicken the waist.
  • Lifting changes the shape: upper chest, wide side delts and bigger arms make the chest look tighter and the waist smaller as fat drops.
  • Targets: ~0.5 kg/week down, 10-12k steps/day floor, 1.6-2 g protein per kg (paneer, dal, soy, curd, whey). Stairs 2-3×/week, 10-15 min.
  • Weigh daily, judge by the weekly average.
