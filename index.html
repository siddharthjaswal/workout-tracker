Training guidelines

  • Repeat the 4-day rotation weekly (A -> B -> C -> D -> repeat), resting or walking between sessions as needed.
  • Rest: 60-75s on compound moves, 30-45s on isolation/core work (see each sheet's Rest column).
  • 5 min warm-up (light cardio + mobility) before every session.
  • Cardio: stair climbing 3-4x/week, 10-15 min - go up hard, come down slow. Flat walks on off days.
  • Listen to soreness: dull, achy muscle soreness is normal (DOMS) - light sessions or a rest day are fine.
  • Sharp or joint-level pain is a stop signal - skip the session and let it settle, don't push through it.
  • Log every session (even walks and rest days) in the 'Workout Log' tab to track consistency over time.

How to use this page

  • Each day tab lists the exercises with sets, reps, suggested weight, rest and a form cue.
  • Form video opens a YouTube demo — the only part that needs an internet connection.
  • The Log tab saves sessions in this browser (offline) and exports to CSV whenever you want.
  • Print or save as PDF from your browser — every day prints on its own, videos and buttons are hidden.
