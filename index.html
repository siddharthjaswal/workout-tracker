Week 1 back from the break

  • 2 sets per exercise instead of 3-4.
  • One dumbbell size down from the listed start (10 → 7.5, 7.5 → 5, 5 stays 5 with fewer reps).
  • Stop every set 3 reps shy of failure. Soreness on day 2-3 is the enemy this week, not the weight.
  • Week 2: full sets, listed weights.

This week

Four sessions, any days, any order. The week resets every Saturday. If you can only do two: Upper A + Lower A.

Placing the sessions

  • Never two lifting sessions on the same day. A day between the two upper sessions (and between the two lower ones) is ideal, not required.
  • Alternate when you can: upper, lower, upper, lower. Mild soreness picks which session, not whether — if the warm-up loosens it, lift; if it doesn’t or anything is sharp, walk instead.
  • Short on time → first 4 exercises, then stop. 30 minutes beats zero.
  • Lift or walk, never both, never neither. Lifting day: whatever steps the day gives you. Walking day: 10k steps, or 15 min of stairs plus a short walk when time is short. Alternate them: lift, walk, lift, walk.
  • Missed sessions don’t roll over. Saturday starts fresh at Upper A.

Rest, simply

  • Every exercise shows Rest between sets. Finish a set, tap it, a countdown appears at the bottom and buzzes when it’s time. That is the only rest you take.
  • Between exercises: the same rest as the last set, no more. Set up the bench during it.
  • Big moves get 75-90s, arms and delts 45s, core 30-45s. Longer rest doesn’t make the set better; it makes the session longer.

Progression rule

  • Every exercise lists a start weight and a rep range. 2×10 means 10 kg in each hand, 1×10 one dumbbell.
  • Work inside the range. When every set hits the top of the range with 1-2 reps still in the tank → next dumbbell, reps drop back to the bottom. Type the reps when you hit the top so the app can tell you.
  • Ladder: 5 → 7.5 → 10. At 10 kg and the top of the range, follow the exercise’s Progress line (slower lowering, pauses, 1.5 reps, harder variation, extra set).
  • Form first. A rep that needs a swing, a shrug or an arch doesn’t count.
  • Presses and squats will cap out at 10 kg within ~6-8 weeks. A 12.5 kg pair is the single best upgrade when that happens.

Fat loss, honestly

  • Love handles, belly and chest fat come off through a calorie deficit + daily steps, not through exercises aimed at them. No weighted side bends — they thicken the waist.
  • Lifting changes the shape: upper chest, wide side delts and bigger arms make the chest look tighter and the waist smaller as fat drops.
  • Targets: ~0.5 kg/week down, 3-4 walking days a week at 10k between the lifting days, 1.6-2 g protein per kg (paneer, dal, soy, curd, whey).
  • Weigh daily, judge by the weekly average.

Using this page

  • Open a session and tap Done under each exercise as you finish it — the tap time is the workout time. Modified if you changed weight, sets or reps; Couldn’t if the body gave up. Nothing else is required.
  • When every exercise has a status the session summary writes itself and lands in the Log. Add how you felt or paste watch / sleep stats only if you want to.
  • Watch data (sleep, steps, resting HR, workouts) arrives by itself every morning from Google Health once connected — it shows on each session card and in the Log. For anything else, share text to Workout from Android’s share sheet and it attaches to that day’s session.
  • Form videos play right here (they need internet; everything else works offline). The page remembers which tab you were on.
  • Install it: Chrome on Android → ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.
